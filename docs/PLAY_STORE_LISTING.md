## 3. Data Safety & Privacy Declarations for Play Console

### Ads Declaration
- Select: **"Yes, my app contains ads"** (Google AdMob).

### Data Collection & Sharing (AdMob)
When filling out the Data Safety form in Play Console, declare:
1. **Device or Other IDs**:
   - Data Type: `Device or other IDs` (e.g. Android Advertising ID).
   - Collected: **Yes**
   - Shared: **Yes** (Shared with Google AdMob for advertising and analytics).
   - Ephemeral: No.
   - Purpose: **Advertising or marketing, Analytics**.
2. **App Activity / Diagnostic Data**:
   - Data Type: `App interactions`, `Crash logs`, `Diagnostics`.
   - Collected: **Yes**
   - Shared: **Yes** (For Google Mobile Ads SDK performance monitoring).
3. **Location (Coarse Location)**:
   - Data Type: `Approximate location` (if network-derived location ads are enabled in AdMob).

## 4. Privacy Policy URL
A Privacy Policy URL is required by Google Play when using AdMob and Microphone permissions:
- You can host a markdown or HTML privacy policy on GitHub Pages (e.g., `https://mosamircs-ux.github.io/Concat1/privacy.html`).
- State clearly:
  1. Video and audio editing takes place entirely on the user's device.
  2. Microphone permission is used solely for recording voiceovers inside the app.
  3. Third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) may collect anonymous advertising identifiers to serve ads.
