Privacy Policy

App: Video Editor (com.mosamircs.videoeditor) · Effective Date: November 18, 2026

1. Overview

Video Editor is a local-first video editing application. All video decoding, timeline editing, effects rendering, speech transcription, and video exporting happen 100% directly on your device. We do not require account registration and we never upload your videos, photos, audio recordings, or project files to our servers.

2. Permissions Used

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used strictly when you choose to record a voiceover directly inside your video project. Audio is saved locally on your device only.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to download optional on-device AI models (when requested by you) and to display ads via Google AdMob.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

This app displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use non-personal device information, including the Android Advertising ID, approximate location, app interaction metrics, and crash/diagnostic data, to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK can manage or withdraw advertising consent at any time. You may also reset or opt out of personalized ads through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

4. Data Security & Retention

Because your media projects remain stored exclusively on your device, you have full control over their retention and deletion. Any diagnostic or advertising data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

5. Children's Privacy

Video Editor is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Video Editor Google Play Store page.
